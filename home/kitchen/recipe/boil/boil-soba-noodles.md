# How to Boil Soba Noodles (Buckwheat Noodles)

## 🍜 Ingredients
- Soba noodles (dry or fresh)
- Water (plenty, at least 4–5 cups per serving)

---

## 🔥 Cooking Steps

### 1. Bring water to a boil
- Use a large pot
- Bring water to a **rolling boil**

---

### 2. Add noodles
- Add soba noodles to boiling water
- Stir gently to prevent sticking

---

### 3. Cook noodles
- Cook for **3–5 minutes** (check package instructions)
- Stir occasionally

➡️ Noodles are ready when:
- Tender but still slightly firm (al dente)
- No hard core inside

---

### 4. Drain immediately
- Pour into a colander

---

### 5. Rinse with cold water (important)
- Rinse thoroughly under cold running water
- Gently rub noodles to remove excess starch

➡️ This step:
- Stops cooking
- Improves texture (firmer, less sticky)

---

## ❄️ For Cold Soba (Recommended)

- Rinse until noodles are completely cool
- Drain well before serving

---

## 🔥 For Hot Soba

- Skip long rinsing (optional quick rinse only)
- Serve immediately in broth or stir-fry

---

## 🧠 Key Tips

- Use plenty of water to avoid sticking
- Do not overcook (soba becomes soft quickly)
- Always rinse for best texture (especially for cold dishes)

---

## ❌ Common Mistakes

- Overcooking → mushy noodles
- Not rinsing → sticky texture
- Too little water → clumping

---

## ✅ Result

- Smooth, slightly firm noodles
- Clean taste
- Non-sticky texture

---

## 🍽 Serving Ideas

- Cold with dipping sauce
- Tossed with light soy-based dressing
- Served alongside stir-fried dishes
