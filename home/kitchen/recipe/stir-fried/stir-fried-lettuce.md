# Stir-Fried Lettuce (Quick & Simple Guide)

## 🥬 Ingredients
- Lettuce (1 head, separated into large pieces)
- Garlic (2–3 cloves, minced or smashed)
- Salt (to taste)
- Soy sauce (optional, small amount)
- Cooking oil

---

## 🔪 Preparation

1. **Wash the lettuce**
    - Rinse thoroughly.
    - **Drain well** (very important to avoid excess water when cooking).

2. **Prepare garlic**
    - Smash or mince.

3. **Do NOT cut lettuce too small**
    - Keep pieces large for better texture.

---

## 🔥 Cooking Steps (Total time: ~1 minute)

### 1. Heat the pan
- Heat the pan until hot.
- Add oil (slightly more than usual for better texture).

---

### 2. Add garlic
- Stir-fry garlic for about **10 seconds**
- Do not burn it

---

### 3. Add lettuce
- Add all lettuce at once
- Turn heat to **high**
- Stir quickly

---

### 4. Season & finish
- Add salt
- Optional: a small splash of soy sauce
- Stir for **20–30 seconds**

➡️ Remove immediately when:
- Leaves are just wilted
- Color is bright green

---

## 🧠 Key Tips

- Use **high heat**
- Cook **very quickly**
- Do NOT overcook
- Make sure lettuce is **dry before cooking**

---

## ❌ Common Mistakes

- Lettuce too wet → watery result
- Low heat → no flavor
- Overcooking → soft, soggy texture

---

## ✅ Result

- Bright green color
- Lightly wilted but still crisp
- Garlicky aroma
- Clean, fresh taste

---

## 🍽 Serving Tip

Serve immediately while hot.  
Pairs well with rice or any stir-fried meat dish.
