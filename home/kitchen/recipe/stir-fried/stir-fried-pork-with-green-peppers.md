# Stir-Fried Pork with Green Peppers (Quick Guide)

## 🥩🌶 Ingredients
- Pork (200g, thinly sliced; shoulder or leg)
- Green peppers (2–3, thinly sliced)
- Garlic (2–3 cloves, minced)
- Soy sauce
- Salt
- Cornstarch (optional, for tenderness)
- Cooking oil

---

## 🔪 Preparation

### 1. Marinate the pork (key step)
- Add to pork:
    - 1 tsp soy sauce
    - 1 tsp cornstarch (optional)
    - 1 tbsp water
- Mix well and let sit for **5 minutes**

---

### 2. Prepare vegetables
- Slice green peppers into strips
- Remove seeds if preferred (less spicy)

---

## 🔥 Cooking Steps (Total time: ~2–3 minutes)

### 1. Cook the pork
- Heat pan until hot
- Add oil
- Add pork and quickly stir to separate
- Cook until **just turned pale (about 10–20 seconds)**

➡️ Remove immediately and set aside

---

### 2. Cook the peppers
- Add a little more oil if needed
- Add garlic, stir for 10 seconds
- Add green peppers
- Stir-fry on high heat for **30–40 seconds**

➡️ Slight blistering/wrinkling = better flavor

---

### 3. Combine & season
- Return pork to pan
- Add:
    - 1 tsp soy sauce
    - Salt to taste
- Stir-fry for **15–20 seconds**

➡️ Remove immediately

---

## 🧠 Key Tips

- Slice pork thin for tenderness
- Do not overcook pork (it will become tough)
- Use high heat throughout
- Cook pork and vegetables separately first
- Total cooking time should be short

---

## ❌ Common Mistakes

- Overcooking pork → dry and tough
- Too much soy sauce → overly salty and dark
- Low heat → no aroma (“wok hei”)

---

## ✅ Result

- Tender pork
- Slightly crisp peppers
- Balanced savory flavor
- Light oil sheen and aroma

---

## 🍽 Serving Tip

Serve hot with rice or noodles.  
Pairs well with simple greens like stir-fried lettuce.
